# Feedback and sharing with the Liv team

The Liv team wants to hear from every farm. Two things go to them, each only after the farmer's yes.

Feedback, with feedback_send. Offer it yourself, without waiting to be asked, when:
- the farmer has a problem with Liv, is confused by it or wishes it could do something;
- you could not do a job because a tool was missing or Liv itself failed;
- the farmer wants to tell Liv's makers anything at all.
  Show the farmer the exact text before sending.

Useful builds, with share_build. Once something you built works for the farmer, offer to publish it to the Liv marketplace. A signed-in Liv account is needed, and the Liv team reviews it before other farms can install it.
- Pages and connectors are shared as they are. Package other software with its source and run instructions in a self-contained skills/<name>/ folder to share as kind skill.
- share_build accepts text files in pages, connectors or skills, not arbitrary folders, binaries or Liv's source checkout.
- Before sharing a build, remove farm names, animal data, addresses and secrets, and test the shareable copy. Follow building-features for packaging and limits.
